No. 9 Canyon del Oro was victorious against No. 8 Pueblo in a first-round matchup that ended in three sets on Thursday, November 6, 2026.

Canyon del Oro set the tone from the get-go, dominating the opening set despite being the away team.

## Brown Sisters Lead the Dorados

The outside hitter duo of Rilynn Brown and Ava Brown has totaled **561 combined kills** this season, making them the top two attacking players for the Canyon del Oro Dorados.

This game was no different, with the Brown sisters taking control from the first set.

Ava Brown spoke on the key factors behind the Dorados' dominant start.

> "We came out with a lot of power and fight and we were here to win," Ava said. "We practiced really hard the last few days, we knew we had to come out here and fight and give it our all."

## Fighting for a Playoff Spot

Canyon del Oro fought to secure a playoff spot this year, beginning the week with a play-in game against Lake Havasu.

Rilynn Brown put on a show with **12 kills** in the Dorados' 3-0 victory, helping the team advance into the state tournament.

Rilynn spoke on how she handles the pressure of a win-or-go-home scenario.

> "Leave it on the court," Rilynn said. "If you know this might be your last time playing you've got to give it your all."

## Canyon del Oro Advances to the Quarterfinals

The **No. 9 Dorados** secured a **3-0 victory** over No. 8 Pueblo, advancing to the quarterfinals of the 5A state volleyball tournament.

Canyon del Oro will face **No. 1 Notre Dame Prep** on Tuesday, November 10, in a quarterfinal matchup with a spot in the state semifinals on the line.
