Ironwood Ridge faced off against Mica Mountain on Tuesday, Oct. 6, securing a **three-set victory** and strengthening its position for a top divisional playoff seed.

## Quiri Leads the Nighthawks

Ashlyn Quiri, senior outside hitter, led the Nighthawks to victory with **22 kills and two blocks**, a performance that highlighted the team's strong form heading into the November playoffs.

Quiri spoke about the importance of facing strong competition ahead of the state championships.

> "Playing a team like Mica is good preparation for the state championships because it's how our competition is going to be," Quiri said. "We are the best team in 4A right now, and I think we showed it on the court."

## George Reaches 1,000 Career Kills

Kallie George, junior outside hitter, reached a major milestone by recording her **1,000th career kill** while adding 14 kills to the Nighthawks' offense.

George emphasized the importance of defeating a competitive opponent like Mica Mountain.

> "I think it was definitely a big win for us and a big confidence booster," George said. "We are looking to make it to the state championship this year, and we are excited to get it all together by the end of playoffs."

The duo of George and Quiri combined for **36 kills**, continuing their dominance in the 4A division.

George also spoke about having Quiri as a teammate.

> "She's a big supporter, and she definitely raises my competitive game," George said. "She just helps us with leadership."

## Mica Mountain's Standout Performance

Aubrey Necas, senior outside hitter for Mica Mountain, was a bright spot for her team, recording **20 kills and three blocks** while continuing her impressive senior season.

## Rematch Set for October 29

Ironwood Ridge and Mica Mountain are scheduled to meet again on **Thursday, Oct. 29**, in what will be the final regular-season game for both teams.
