No. 16 Marana Football will face No. 1 Desert Mountain in the quarterfinals of the 5A state tournament after defeating Sunnyslope **45-35** on Friday, November 6, 2026.

The Tigers enter the matchup following an impressive offensive performance, but they now face one of the most dominant defensive teams in Arizona.

## Howell and Hicks Lead Marana's Passing Attack

Receiver Jesse Howell and quarterback Trenton Hicks connected for **three touchdowns** in the Tigers' first-round victory over Sunnyslope.

Their connection played a major role in Marana's offensive success, helping the Tigers advance to the quarterfinals.

Howell made it clear that Marana is ready for the challenge ahead, delivering a message about what Desert Mountain should expect.

> "Just be ready; we're coming," Howell said.

## Jeffery Smith III Sets the Tone for the Tigers

Running back Jeffery Smith III has been a dominant presence for Marana all season on both sides of the field, averaging **123 rushing yards per game and 13 tackles per game**.

In the first round of the 5A state tournament, Smith delivered an outstanding performance, rushing for **208 yards on 20 carries**.

Smith spoke about the physicality he brought to the Tigers' first-round victory.

> "I have to set the tone," Smith III said. "They were closing out on that run, and I had to punish them."

His ability to establish a physical running game will be crucial as Marana prepares to face Desert Mountain's disciplined defense.

## Desert Mountain's Dominant Defense

Desert Mountain has been electric throughout the season, with its only loss coming from a forfeit to Arizona College Prep.

The Wolves feature a disciplined offense that has committed only **four turnovers**, along with arguably one of the best defenses in Arizona.

No opponent has scored more than **16 points** against Desert Mountain's defense this season.

Cornerback Hassan Smith, middle linebacker Christian McComber, and defensive end/tight end Davian Whitener have dominated the 5A division with their defensive presence.

Their ability to disrupt opposing offenses will present a major challenge for Marana's explosive offensive attack.

## Marana's Keys to Victory

For Marana to pull off the upset against the No. 1 seed, the Tigers must establish their physical presence early.

**Force Desert Mountain to respect the run.** Jeffery Smith III must continue his dominant rushing performances, forcing the Wolves to commit defenders to stopping the ground game.

**Create explosive passing plays.** With Desert Mountain focused on the run, Trenton Hicks and Jesse Howell must capitalize on opportunities to break off chunk plays through the air.

**Pressure the Wolves' offense.** Marana must disrupt Desert Mountain's offensive rhythm from the opening drive, applying consistent pressure and forcing turnovers.

## Desert Mountain's Keys to Victory

Desert Mountain will look to continue its defensive dominance while controlling the tempo of the matchup.

**Control the offensive pace.** The Wolves must establish their rhythm early, sustaining drives and wearing down Marana's defense.

**Limit Marana's explosive plays.** Desert Mountain must contain Jeffery Smith III on the ground while preventing Hicks and Howell from connecting on deep passes.

**Apply constant defensive pressure.** The Wolves' defensive front must disrupt Marana's offense, forcing difficult decisions and limiting opportunities for big plays.

## A Quarterfinal Showdown

Marana enters the quarterfinals looking to continue its postseason run after an impressive **45-35 victory** over Sunnyslope.

Standing in the Tigers' way is a No. 1 Desert Mountain team that has consistently shut down opposing offenses throughout the season.

With Marana's explosive offensive playmakers facing Desert Mountain's elite defense, this 5A state quarterfinal matchup presents a major test for both programs.
