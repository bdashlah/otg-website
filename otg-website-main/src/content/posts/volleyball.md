## Ironwood Ridge
![Ironwood Ridge Volleyball](/images/ironridge.jpg)
**Best Wins:** Mica Mountain, Saguaro, CDO, Sahuarita, Sunrise Mountain

**Overall Record:** 19-5 (According to AZPreps)

**Region Record:** 12-0

Finishing first in the 4A Kino Region with an undefeated record, Ironwood Ridge looks like one of the most polished and composed teams coming out of Arizona.

Ironwood Ridge has been elite offensively all year. The outside hitter duo of Ashlyn Quiri, senior, and Kallie George, junior, combine for around ten kills a set. Quiri and George lead Arizona for most combined total kills as a duo for the year.

Setter Marcella Landers, sophomore, averages nine assists a set this year which ranks in the top five of Arizona rankings.

In tough matchups against teams like Mica Mountain and Saguaro, this team finds ways to win by dictating the pace of games early from start to finish.

Ironwood Ridge is a title-contender through and through.

## Salpointe Catholic

**Best Wins:** Canyon del Oro, Sunrise Mountain

**Overall Record:** 23-11 (According to AZPreps)

**Region Record:** 12-0

Salpointe Catholic finished their season undefeated in the 5A Sonoran Region.

This is a program that won a state championship last year, but twelve players have graduated from that roster.

That being said, Salpointe Catholic has still found success this year led by setter and opposite side hitter Callen Hutchens, senior, who averages 2.4 kills per set with sixty-four serving aces on the year.

Salpointe's libero, Reagan Burgle, junior, adds 427 digs on the year helping keep plays alive.

This team wins games through a balanced approach and the tenacity to win close sets.

With wins against Canyon del Oro and Sunrise Mountain, Salpointe Catholic has proven they can make a playoff run.

## Mica Mountain
![Mica Mountain Volleyball](/images/mica-mountain.jpg)

**Best Wins:** Buena, Sahuarita

**Overall Record:** 14-3 (According to AZPreps)

**Region Record:** 9-3

Mica Mountain finished their season 9-3 in the 4A Kino Region with all three losses being to solid opponents.

This team is one of the most explosive in Arizona with non-stop energy.

Mica Mountain has strong players on both offense and defense this year.

Outside hitter Aubrey Necas, senior, has been the leader for this team all year with five kills a game and the highest hitting percentage in the 4A Kino Region.

Middle blocker Mckenna Mallard, sophomore, has eighty total blocks on the year which ranks third in the 4A division.

Mica Mountain wins games by pushing a quicker tempo and using their energy to overwhelm opponents.

With wins against Buena and Sahuarita, Mica Mountain has proved they can make a deep playoff run this November.

## Pueblo
![Pueblo Volleyball](/images/pueblo.jpg)
**Best Wins:** Buena, Cienega

**Overall Record:** 22-4 (According to AZPreps)

**Region Record:** 11-1

Pueblo finished their season first in the 5A Southern Region with a record of 11-1.

This team has a roster full of talent that have dominated the 5A Division.

Outside hitter Isabella Carrillo, senior, has been lights out all year leading the 5A division in both kills (356) and digs (491).

Outside hitter Brooklyn Rosthenhausler-Espinoza, senior, has also been elite offensively with 343 kills this year ranking second behind Carrillo in the 5A Division.

Setter Anette Urquijo, senior, has aided Carrillo and Rosthenhausler-Espinoza with 641 total assists which ranks in the top five of the 5A Division.

Pueblo is an experienced team that has multiple weapons offensively and defensively.

Their wins come from consistency and discipline that can be found in their upperclassmen stars.

If their upperclassmen continue performing the way they have throughout the season, Pueblo could go far in the 5A State Tournament.
