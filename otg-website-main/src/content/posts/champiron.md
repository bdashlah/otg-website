Ironwood Ridge and Mica Mountain put on a show for the third time this season, representing Southern Arizona in the 4A State Championship Final on Saturday, November 14, at Arizona Veterans' Memorial Coliseum.

Ironwood Ridge finished the year as **state champions**, defeating Mica Mountain **3-1** to secure the program's first-ever state championship.

## Quiri and George Lead the Nighthawks to Victory

Outside hitters Ashlyn Quiri and Kallie George combined for a total of **46 kills**, with Quiri recording a career-high **28 kills** in a single match, along with 15 digs.

Quiri and George had spoken about their playoff expectations since their first matchup with Mica Mountain back in October.

> "We're hungry for the state championship, so I think we hold each other accountable and we just really want it this year; we want to do it for (Coach) Lang," Quiri said.

In the Nighthawks' final regular-season game on October 30, George also emphasized the hunger this team had for playoff success.

> "We're so ready. All the teams want it, but honestly we want it really bad this year; we want it more."

George backed up those words with an impressive performance during the 4A state championship, contributing **18 kills and 13 digs** to help secure the victory.

## Landers Delivers an Elite Championship Performance

Setter Marcella Landers also delivered an elite performance during the state championship, recording **53 assists** in a single match.

Landers has been one of the top setters in the 4A division, finishing the regular season among the top five players on the division's assists leaderboard.

Her performance helped power the Nighthawks' offensive attack, providing opportunities for Quiri and George to dominate at the net.

## A Historic Senior Season Comes to an End

Ironwood Ridge ended the season on a remarkable **19-game winning streak**, bringing Quiri's historic senior season as a Nighthawk to a close.

Quiri, the all-time kills leader in Southern Arizona, will continue her volleyball career at **Florida State University** as a Seminole.

Other seniors who played their final game as Nighthawks include:

- **Kendyl Shields** — Outside Hitter (OH)
- **Madeline Flowers** — Libero (L)
- **Lilly Merchant** — Middle Blocker (MB)

These seniors helped lead Ironwood Ridge to a historic season, leaving behind a championship legacy for the program.

## Ironwood Ridge Makes Program History

Finishing with a **19-5 overall record**, Ironwood Ridge Volleyball cemented itself as the top 4A team in Arizona.

The Nighthawks' victory over Mica Mountain marked the program's **first-ever state championship**, capping off a historic season defined by determination, teamwork, and an unforgettable 19-game winning streak.

For Ironwood Ridge, the season ended with more than just a victory. It ended with a place in program history.
