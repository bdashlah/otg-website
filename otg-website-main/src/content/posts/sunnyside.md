Sunnyside Boys Soccer is looking to build on its impressive **21-2 record** from the 2025-26 season, with hopes of finding early success and making a deep playoff run during the 2026-27 campaign.

The team shared its expectations for the upcoming season during the 2026 Sunnyside Winter Sports Media Day on November 10, 2026.

Team representatives Brian Bacahui (MF, Sr.), Israel Luquez (MF, Sr.), Ruben Arballo (D/MF, Sr.), and Fernando Jimenez-Vasquez (D, Sr.) spoke about the offseason preparation that has given the team confidence heading into the winter.

## Bacahui Highlights Sunnyside's Offseason Preparation

Senior midfielder Brian Bacahui contributed **seven goals and 10 assists in 10 games** during the 2025-26 season.

Heading into the 2026-27 campaign, Bacahui discussed the offseason training implemented by head coach Casey O'Brien, particularly the team's focus on performing under pressure late in games.

> "We've definitely been focusing on those last moments of a game where you're very tired and a little bit sluggish," Bacahui said. "Coach O'Brien calls those the winning moments."

When asked about the results of the offseason preparation, Bacahui described the development of Sunnyside's playing style over the past few years.

> "(We're) very compact; we've been working on it for well over three years already," Bacahui said. "We like to touch the ball a lot, we like to communicate on the field, and when we lose the ball we all drop back and make sure to get it back."

The team's emphasis on possession, communication, and defensive organization will remain central to its approach this season.

## Luquez Looks to Turn Offseason Work Into Results

Senior midfielder Israel Luquez was an offensive dual-threat for Sunnyside during the 2025-26 season, recording **eight goals and 15 assists in 13 games**.

Luquez spoke about the intensity of the team's offseason training and how that preparation could translate into success during the regular season.

> "We've been focusing on hard work, and right now we're still in the offseason; we're still working hard," Luquez said. "All this hard work will hopefully translate to the field."

With his ability to both create scoring opportunities and finish attacks, Luquez will be an important part of Sunnyside's offensive production.

## Arballo Believes Sunnyside Has Championship Potential

Ruben Arballo enters the season as one of Sunnyside's key offensive contributors after scoring **nine goals in eight games** during the 2025-26 campaign.

Arballo spoke about the expectations created by the team's offseason preparation and the confidence surrounding the upcoming season.

> "Showing how hard we worked and showing that we're capable of doing it this year," Arballo said. "We have the talent, the team, and the heart to do it (winning the state championship)."

With a talented returning group and a strong emphasis on preparation, Sunnyside hopes to turn that confidence into postseason success.

## Jimenez-Vasquez Sets Sights on a Deep Playoff Run

Senior defender and newly appointed captain Fernando Jimenez-Vasquez also discussed the team's priorities heading into the season.

For Jimenez-Vasquez, the goal is not simply to repeat last year's success but to push further into the state playoffs.

> "The main focus is making it past the quarter-finals (and) trying to make a mark to show people we are capable of making it past that and the state championship."

His leadership will be important as Sunnyside looks to take the next step toward competing for a state title.

## Sunnyside Prepares for Season Opener

Sunnyside Boys Soccer will begin its 2026-27 campaign at the **Brandon Bean Tournament**, hosted by Salpointe Catholic High School.

The team is scheduled to face **Canyon del Oro on Monday, November 30**, in its first matchup of the season.

After finishing the previous season with a **21-2 record**, Sunnyside enters the new campaign determined to build on that success and establish itself as a state championship contender.
